A good investment?
An easy question, really hard to answer!

  • Exceptional performance, low risk, high return?
    • According to Jordà et al. (2019) residential real estate offers high returns and low risk—they speak of a “house risk premium puzzle”.
  • With better data and more careful estimation, the picture gets less rosy
    • Eichholtz et al. (RFS, 2021) or Chambers et al. (RFS, 2021) show Sharpe ratios are significantly lower when measuring returns at asset level.
